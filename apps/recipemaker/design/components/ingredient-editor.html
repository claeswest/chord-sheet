Ingredients

A fixed quantity column is what makes a list scannable mid-cook. Ragged quantities force the eye to hunt for each number.

Edit

Quantity may be empty. "To taste" and "a pinch" are real ingredients. An em dash holds the column so the list stays aligned, and the qualifier goes in the note. Never force a number where the cook wouldn't use one.
