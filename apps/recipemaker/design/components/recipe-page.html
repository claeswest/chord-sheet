Mormors pannkakor

The thin Swedish kind. The batter wants to rest — that's the whole trick.

Serves
4
Prep
10 min
Cook
20 min
Total
30 min

Ingredients

3 dlplain flour
½ tspsalt
6 dlmilk
3eggs, lightly beaten
50 gbutter, melted

Method

  1. Whisk the flour, salt and half the milk to a smooth batter.
  2. Add the rest of the milk and the eggs. Stir in the melted butter.
  3. Let it rest 30 minutes — this is what makes them thin.
  4. Fry thin in a hot buttered pan until golden at the edges.

Notes

  • Serve with lingonberry jam.
  • Batter keeps overnight in the fridge.
