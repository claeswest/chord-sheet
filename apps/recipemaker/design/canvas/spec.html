Canvas spec

What the AI may decide about a recipe's appearance, and what it may not. Total visual freedom produces unreadable recipes; the constraints below are what keep a styled recipe usable in a kitchen.

Style contract

Stored in Recipe.style. These are the only knobs — anything not listed is structure and is not the AI's to change.

TokenControls
--c-bgPage colour, or the base under a background image
--c-bg-imageGenerated background, optional
--c-ink / --c-mutedPrimary and secondary text
--c-accentSection marks, rules, ornament
--c-qtyQuantities and units
--c-ruleHairlines between ingredients
--c-display / --c-bodyTitle face and reading face

Fixed — not the AI's to change

Why
Ingredient column alignmentA ragged quantity column can't be scanned mid-cook
Step numberingPeople track their place by number, not by paragraph
Order of sectionsIngredients before method, always — you shop before you cook
Body text below 16pxRead at arm's length from a worktop
Line length beyond ~40remLong measures lose the reader between lines

Hard constraints

Contrast is enforced in code, not requested in the prompt

Body text must clear 4.5:1 against its background, and quantities must clear it too. A model asked nicely will still produce sage-on-cream that fails. Clamp the generated values on the way in and reject the style if it can't be fixed.

passes3 dl plain flour — readable at a glance
fails3 dl plain flour — moody, unusable

Quantities stay visually distinct from names

Different colour, weight, or both. This is the one distinction that makes a recipe usable while your hands are busy. If a palette can't support it, the palette is wrong.

Every style must survive print

PDF export and, later, print-on-demand both mean a canvas can end up on paper. A dark background costs ink and reads badly. Either generate a print variant alongside the screen one, or keep backgrounds light enough to print — decide before the styling feature ships, not after someone orders a book.

A recipe with no style is not a broken recipe

Styling is additive. The default canvas must be good on its own, because it is what every recipe looks like before the AI runs, if generation fails, and if the user turns it off.
