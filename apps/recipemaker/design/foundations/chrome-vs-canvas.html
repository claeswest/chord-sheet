Chrome vs canvas

Two surfaces with opposite jobs. The chrome is the app and never changes. The canvas is the recipe, and the AI restyles it per recipe — different fonts, colours and background every time.

Chrome

Fixed. Uses the design tokens.

  • Landing page
  • Library and cards
  • Editor controls and inputs
  • Buttons, dialogs, navigation
  • Login, account, admin

Canvas

AI-styled per recipe. Ignores the tokens.

  • Recipe title and description
  • Ingredient list
  • Method steps
  • Notes and source
  • Background, ornament, palette
